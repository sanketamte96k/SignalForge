## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 24G.17 reported an exact binomial test p-value of:
$$p = 3.24 \times 10^{-8} \ll 0.001$$
based on 59 winning trades out of 73 pooled validation trades across four equities (`ADANIPORTS`, `RELIANCE`, `INFY`, and `ICICIBANK`).

This independent audit rigorously evaluates the validity of this inference. The central question is whether pooling trade outcomes across four distinct equities satisfies the fundamental assumption of **identically and independently distributed (i.i.d.) observations**.

### Key Statistical Findings:
1. **The Reported Pooled Binomial Test Assumes Pseudo-Replication**:
   - The exact binomial test treats the 73 validation trades as 73 independent coin tosses.
   - In reality, these trades are generated by only **$K = 4$ assets** operating within the same macroeconomic regime (NSE large-cap equities in 2019).
   - Pooling cross-sectional observations without adjusting for asset-level random effects or temporal clustering vastly overstates the effective sample size ($N_{\text{eff}} \ll 73$) and artificially compresses standard errors.
2. **Individual Asset Confidence Intervals Cross Zero on 3 of 4 Equities**:
   - At the individual asset level, 3 out of 4 equities have 95% bootstrap confidence intervals for Validation Net P&L that cross zero:
     - `ADANIPORTS`: [-₹4,747.63, +₹8,114.15], Probability of net loss = **25.41%**
     - `INFY`: [-₹5,868.58, +₹11,049.42], Probability of net loss = **24.32%**
     - `ICICIBANK`: [-₹1,121.69, +₹10,957.64], Probability of net loss = **4.16%**
   - Only `RELIANCE` has a 95% CI strictly bounded above zero: [+₹1,458.24, +₹14,310.63], Probability of net loss = **1.01%**.
3. **Dependence-Aware Cluster and Block Resampling**:
   - Moving from Naive IID Bootstrap to Trading-Day Cluster and Temporal Block Bootstrap inflates the standard error of Pooled Net P&L by up to **+16.1%** (SE increases from ₹7,196.36 to ₹8,356.83).
   - The 10-day block bootstrap 95% confidence interval for Net P&L widens to `[+₹3,575.95, +₹36,245.50]`.
4. **Conclusion**:
   - While the point estimates are positive and the pooled portfolio is net profitable in 2019, claiming conclusive statistical significance ($p = 3.24 \times 10^{-8}$) is mathematically unjustified due to unmodeled asset clustering and small cluster count ($K = 4$).
   - The correct statistical classification is **`STATISTICALLY_INCONCLUSIVE`**.

## 2. Resampling Methodology & Parameter Specification

To test the sensitivity of the validation results to temporal and cross-sectional dependence, four bootstrap resampling schemes were executed:

### Methodological Specifications:
- **Resampling Iterations:** $B = 10,000$ iterations per scheme.
- **Random Seed:** $42$ (Deterministic NumPy `np.random.default_rng(42)`).
- **Confidence Interval Method:** Empirical Percentile Bootstrap (2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the empirical bootstrap distribution).
- **Universe Analyzed:** 73 realized validation trades across `ADANIPORTS` (14), `RELIANCE` (21), `INFY` (26), and `ICICIBANK` (12).

### Resampling Architectures:

1. **Scheme 1: Naive IID Trade Bootstrap**
   - *Resampling Unit:* Individual realized trade $(N = 73)$.
   - *Assumption:* Trades are completely independent across time and across symbols.
   - *Procedure:* Draw 73 trades with replacement from the pooled pool of 73 trades.

2. **Scheme 2: Trading-Day Cluster Bootstrap**
   - *Resampling Unit:* Calendar trading day $(N_{\text{days}} = 261$ business days in 2019).
   - *Structure:* All trades that entered on the same calendar date are sampled as an indivisible cluster.
   - *Procedure:* Resample 261 calendar trading days with replacement. Aggregate all trades belonging to the sampled dates. Preserves simultaneous multi-asset correlations.

3. **Scheme 3: 5-Day Temporal Block Bootstrap (Weekly Blocks)**
   - *Resampling Unit:* Contiguous non-overlapping 5-business-day blocks $(N_{\text{blocks}} = 53)$.
   - *Structure:* Captures auto-correlated volatility regimes, momentum clusters, and weekly seasonality.
   - *Procedure:* Resample 53 weekly blocks with replacement. Concatenate all trading days and their constituent trades.

4. **Scheme 4: 10-Day Temporal Block Bootstrap (Fortnightly Blocks)**
   - *Resampling Unit:* Contiguous non-overlapping 10-business-day blocks $(N_{\text{blocks}} = 27)$.
   - *Structure:* Captures extended regime persistence and macroeconomic news drift.
   - *Procedure:* Resample 27 fortnightly blocks with replacement. Concatenate trading days and trades.

## 5. Effective Sample Size & Intra-Cluster Correlation

When observations are grouped within clusters (here, assets and trading days), the **effective sample size** $N_{\text{eff}}$ is defined as:
$$N_{\text{eff}} = \frac{N}{1 + (m - 1)\rho}$$
where:
- $N = 73$ total trades.
- $m \approx \frac{73}{4} = 18.25$ average trades per asset cluster.
- $\rho$ is the intra-cluster correlation coefficient.

### Effective Degrees of Freedom Analysis:
1. **Asset Clustering ($K = 4$):**
   - Because all trades originate from just 4 equities, the maximum degrees of freedom for cross-sectional inference is bounded by $K - 1 = 3$.
   - Any statistical test that claims degrees of freedom proportional to $N = 73$ commits a classical **Type I error inflation** (pseudo-replication).
   - In financial econometrics, standard errors clustered by asset or multi-way clustered by asset and time require asymptotic cluster counts (typically $K \ge 30–50$) for Wald or $t$-tests to achieve nominal coverage. With $K = 4$, cluster-robust asymptotic approximations break down.
2. **Temporal Independence vs Asset Independence:**
   - On 86% of active trading days, only one stock executed a trade. Thus, contemporaneous trade correlation is small ($|r| < 0.075$).
   - However, **asset-level parameter response** is persistent: `RELIANCE` and `ICICIBANK` consistently generate large winners due to high intraday volatility, whereas `INFY` and `ADANIPORTS` generate smaller percentage moves where fixed statutory friction dominates.

## 6. Limitations & Methodological Constraints

1. **Small Cluster Count ($K = 4$):**
   - Four equities are insufficient to establish universe-wide generalizability across the 50 stocks of the NIFTY 50.
2. **Short Validation Window (1 Calendar Year, 2019):**
   - 2019 was characterized by a distinct large-cap polarization regime in Indian equities (where NIFTY was driven by a narrow basket of 5–6 stocks, predominantly Reliance and ICICI Bank).
   - Evaluating only 2019 risks confounding strategy alpha with regime-specific beta concentration.
3. **Winner Concentration Fragility:**
   - Removing just the top 3 winners flips both `ADANIPORTS` and `INFY` into net losses in 2019. The strategy's survival on these two stocks depends on fewer than 4 trades.
