## 2. Event Detailed Analysis

### RECON_2017_01: Effective 2017-03-31
- **Authority:** IISL / NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2017-02-27
- **Description:** Semi-annual review of broad market indices based on data ending Jan 2017.
- **Exclusions:** BHEL, IDEA
- **Inclusions:** IBULHSGFIN, IOC

### CORP_2017_02: Effective 2017-05-26
- **Authority:** IISL / NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2017-04-27
- **Description:** Exclusion of Grasim due to Aditya Birla Nuvo merger and financial services demerger scheme; replaced by Vedanta.
- **Exclusions:** GRASIM
- **Inclusions:** VEDL

### RECON_2017_03: Effective 2017-09-29
- **Authority:** IISL / NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2017-08-28
- **Description:** Semi-annual review. Removal of 3 companies and 1 DVR security (TATAMTRDVR) to restore exactly 50 companies / 50 securities.
- **Exclusions:** ACC, BANKBARODA, TATAPOWER, TATAMTRDVR
- **Inclusions:** BAJFINANCE, HPCL, UPL

### RECON_2018_01: Effective 2018-04-02
- **Authority:** IISL / NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2018-02-27
- **Description:** Semi-annual review. 3 exclusions and 3 inclusions (including Grasim re-entry).
- **Exclusions:** AMBUJACEM, AUROPHARMA, BOSCHLTD
- **Inclusions:** BAJAJFINSV, GRASIM, TITAN

### RECON_2018_02: Effective 2018-09-28
- **Authority:** NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2018-08-28
- **Description:** Semi-annual review. Lupin excluded; JSW Steel included.
- **Exclusions:** LUPIN
- **Inclusions:** JSWSTEEL

### RECON_2019_01: Effective 2019-03-29
- **Authority:** NSE Indices Limited
- **Announcement Date:** 2019-02-25
- **Description:** Semi-annual review. HPCL excluded; Britannia Industries included.
- **Exclusions:** HPCL
- **Inclusions:** BRITANNIA

### RECON_2019_02: Effective 2019-09-27
- **Authority:** NSE Indices Limited

- **Exclusions:** IBULHSGFIN
